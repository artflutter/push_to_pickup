---
title: The vendor.
notes: |
  Walk down the rail. Everything here happens on somebody else's servers,
  before a single byte reaches the phone.
---

<Rail>
  <F><Milestone n="01" title="Contacts your server">A call comes in for a phone number. The vendor asks your backend what to do with it.</Milestone></F>
  <F><Milestone n="02" title="Obtains the user">Your server maps the phone number to a user account.</Milestone></F>
  <F><Milestone n="03" title="Maps the user to a push token">The one the app registered earlier — one per device.</Milestone></F>
  <F><Milestone n="04" title="Emits the push">Off to APNs or FCM. The vendor's part is done.</Milestone></F>
</Rail>
