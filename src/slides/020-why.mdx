<Cards rows={[3, 2]}>
  <F><Card n="01" title="Not a regular task">Nobody tapped anything. The app is dead, the screen is off — and it still has to ring, on time.</Card></F>
  <F><Card n="02" title="Platform specific">iOS: PushKit + CallKit, one mandatory path. Android: FCM + ConnectionService, and every OEM bends the rules.</Card></F>
  <F><Card n="03" title="Metric heavy">Success is a number: time‑to‑ring, answer rate, missed calls. Every hop has a millisecond budget.</Card></F>
  <F><Card n="04" title="Cross‑technological, inside out">Backend → APNs / FCM → OS → native → Dart → widget. Flutter is the last 10% of the pipe, and the only part you can see.</Card></F>
  <F><Card n="05" tone="brand" title="What you build ≠ what ships">On the debug cable it rings in 300 ms. In the wild: Doze, battery savers, killed processes, locked screens. Implementation and outcome drift apart — in both directions.</Card></F>
